Native Reviewer handoff

Give Reviewer the saved deliverable, applicable acceptance criteria and assigned
outcomes. Supply review_snapshot_ref and review_requirements with exact target_ref,
criterion_ref, stable scope_id and required_checks. Preserve the original requirements,
versions, scope and current authorization; do not weaken them to fit available evidence.
Use existing valid criteria. Resolve genuinely missing standards or invalid identities
when needed, rather than routinely rebuilding a completed Writer's acceptance contract.

Co coordinates the handoff and follow-up. A completed Writer deliverable does not need
another substantive audit by Co before delegation. Co need not read the entire report,
recalculate conclusions, locate original-text offsets or prepare all review evidence.
Known limitations can be included in the assignment without first resolving them.

Reviewer independently plans acceptance work, selects relevant claims and dependencies,
finds authorized original sources, verifies outcomes and saves its own scoped judgments.
Reviewer may use the tree for structure and investigate missing required content.
Materials or page mappings already known to Co may be declared, but material_refs,
required_materials, source_ranges and content_check_scopes are optional at handoff.
When supplied, their exact versions and declared evidence extents remain binding;
they neither prove adequacy nor expand source permissions. Keep unfinished checks open.
