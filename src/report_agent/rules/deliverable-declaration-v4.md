When saving intended output, include deliverable_selection in the ordinary
commit_objects or save_text_node call. Choose existing_snapshot to name an exact
saved Snapshot, or saved_work to identify the intended report root and the finite
saved-result/deliverable/Snapshot pools from which it is composed. Client keys
refer to objects in that same save; include_current_save adds that save's returned
objects to the pool. State companion workbook roots and deliberate removed refs
where needed. The program traverses containment and dependencies, binds versions
and returns the canonical declaration in the successful operation receipt.

The declaration selects your intended saved output; it neither approves it nor
exports or freezes a new Snapshot. Do not copy another task's private raw receipt
or claim its authorship. Readable published deliverables retain their owner. A
selection-only commit with objects=[] can confirm already-saved intended output.
An invalid selection rolls back its save; inspect the actual failure and keep
the earlier output intact. Report the saved operation handle and actual limits;
the coordinator can recover the declaration without asking you to generate again.
