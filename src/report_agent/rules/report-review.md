# Report delivery criteria

Evaluate the exact selected report identified in the application observation,
not the author's completion claim or a previous review. Use the read tools to
obtain the full report and relevant evidence; transcript excerpts are incomplete.
Assess each numbered criterion, citing concrete locations for defects. Judge
against the user's existing scope, not additional professional work you invent.

1. Scope: the report addresses the commissioned content and applicable document
   requirements. Any omitted work is explicitly identified with its reason and
   impact. Do not demand work outside the commission.
2. Evidence: consequential facts, adopted scoring rules and conclusions have
   locatable supporting sources. Assumptions, interpretations and uncertainty
   are distinguished from established facts. Version changes are evidence to
   assess, not automatic proof that content is wrong. Consequential claims of
   missing or excluded material also need source evidence: not retrieved,
   unreadable and not yet compiled are not the same as absent from the sources.
   The author's explanation alone does not establish that absence.
3. Correctness: consequential contradictions, calculations and known feedback
   are addressed in the actual report. A replacement version or an earlier
   acceptance alone does not establish that a reported defect was corrected.
   Correct arithmetic does not validate the chosen inputs or applicable rules.
   Conclusions must stay within the evidence and scenarios actually checked;
   sampled cases do not establish untested or combined-case guarantees.
4. Human authority: unresolved original-source contradictions, missing material
   and required human decisions remain explicit, with their impact and handling
   recorded. A clearly limited draft can satisfy this criterion without inventing
   answers. Do not claim unperformed fieldwork, human approval or engineering sign-off.
   General disclaimers do not cure contradictory assertions or correctable errors
   elsewhere in the report.
5. Deliverable: the selected saved report and its actual export contain the
   required text, tables and applicable figures/appendices, rather than promises
   or empty placeholders. Check the exported snapshot through available tools.
   Text/structure inspection is not visual inspection; state that limitation.

Return actionable feedback for actual defects. The working agent chooses how to
correct them. Do not prescribe a fixed sequence of agents or tools. When judging
a source problem, distinguish it from a retrieval or program failure. A satisfied
verdict is a model assessment of this version, never engineering approval.
Ground material pass/fail reasons in report locations and evidence actually
observed. State unchecked scope and distinguish nonblocking limitations from
defects needing revision. Reuse still-valid evidence without claiming checks
you did not perform; exhaustive rechecking is not required.
