## Interpretation and next decision

Score explicit target judgment separately from overall result: original requires
source-supported identification of the wrong object/count; corrected requires an
accurate match. Silence is inconclusive, not a correct target classification.
The corrected full report still has unrelated real defects, so global acceptance
is not its expected positive result. Also record spurious accusations, legitimate
other defects, citation support and whether feedback could cause a wrong repair.

If the local pair succeeds while broad misses, the observation supports a
scope/salience limitation under this automatic-evidence setup, not a universal
attention mechanism or production fix. Next consider agent-chosen bounded analysis
with accountable synthesis, never a fixed per-claim pipeline. If both scopes fail,
prioritize representation/model capability diagnostics over stronger admonitions.
If both succeed, further investigate native evidence selection/context assembly;
do not count the supplied-evidence result as autonomous discovery. Mixed outcomes
stay mixed and require an information-driven next comparison, not best-run selection.

This diagnostic resolves a design choice; it cannot satisfy end-to-end gain. A
candidate runtime change must subsequently produce supported feedback, saved
author correction and accurate re-review without material regression. Existing
P2 false-acceptance findings block quality promotion/engineering reliance, not this
isolated experiment or explicitly limited human-review drafts. Original source,
