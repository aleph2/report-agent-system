## Small implementation

Keep criterion IDs, versions, ownership and existing storage schema. Clarify
coordinator guidance and criterion field descriptions, using the current condition,
evidence_expectation and allowed_limitations fields rather than adding compulsory
checklists. Describe outcome sufficiency, material disqualifying contradictions,
and uncertainty relative to the commission. No universal zero-error or full-scan
rule. No mandatory number of criteria or evidence items. No task-specific known
answers or Task031 defect list in runtime prompts.

Clarify the grader's generic three-state semantics while retaining autonomous
investigation, bounded tools, read-only upstream access and human decisions.
An admissible disclosed source limitation differs from an unsupported definite
assertion, and unresolved verification differs from an established report defect.
Do not change status aggregation or introduce deterministic semantic truth checks.

## Verification declaration

Independent fresh non-engineering synthetic controls cover correct work, material
contradictions, genuine uncertainty and permitted limitations. Expected labels stay
outside model-visible inputs. Preserve complete public inputs/outputs, settings and
tool observations; do not inspect private reasoning. Freeze fixtures and prompts
before calls. GLM-5.3/high and current adapter parameters stay fixed; no vision is
necessary for text-only synthetic fixtures. Record actual choices and usage.

Compare three arms: original criteria + original grader; clearer equivalent criteria
+ original grader; clearer criteria + clarified grader. Counterbalance order and
use two repetitions per independent case. Cases and labels are defined by tester
before live calls, not fitted to results. These are direct native-grader diagnostics,
not evidence of author improvement. Evaluate item-level misses, false rejections,

not evidence. A small all-correct baseline gives no improvement estimate.
