## Evidence and acceptance

Freeze source hashes, original goal, code/config hashes, import receipt and actual
provider/tool events. Report what was actually read/used, discovered, corrected,
missed or incorrectly flagged. A fresh run can avoid historical errors without
discovering an injected error; do not conflate prevention with correction.

Keep evaluation criteria outside the application's tools/inputs. Human report is
comparison evidence only, not ground truth. No same-input causal attribution to a
single prompt change: tools and starting state differ from historical trials.

Success means a materially useful source-grounded report with concrete feedback
and traceable handling of important gaps, not merely completed calls or a model's
accepted label. If a native human interrupt occurs, preserve it and show the exact
decision needed; do not invent an answer to finish the test. State which quality
questions remain unevaluated. Further repairs follow observed causes, not guesses.
