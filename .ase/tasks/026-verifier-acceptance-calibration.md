## Candidate independent variable

Preserve artifact input, grounded_verdict=true, model/parameters, source observations,
native GraderResponse/status meanings and author/re-review loop. Add only this
optional responsibility paragraph to the effective system prompt:

> Assess materiality against the commissioned use and explain the impact.
> Suitability for circulation as a staged draft does not by itself establish that
> each rubric criterion is satisfied. Pending sign-off or methodological confirmation
> cannot justify a known incorrect assertion or reconcile a contradiction. Where a
> material defect prevents a criterion from being met, request correction or an
> accurate limitation of the affected claim in the artifact itself. Disclosed
> uncertainty and minor imperfections may remain when compatible with the commission
> and the claims made.

This specifies the outcome, not an investigation sequence, required pages, tool
count, defect quota or mandatory scan. It does not make every P2 block circulation
or every typo require revision. A general disclaimer is not an accurate limitation
of an affected factual claim. Reviewer findings remain evidence; Main owns delivery
disposition. No engineering sign-off or source/human-decision authority is delegated.
