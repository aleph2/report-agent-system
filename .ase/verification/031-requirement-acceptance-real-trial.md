### User question: are pass/fail standards absent?

Main inspected the captured effective grader system prompt and the criterion input
schema. Overall judgment rules were present: satisfied only when all criteria pass,
needs_revision for any established not_satisfied, and undetermined when unresolved
assessment remains without an established failure. The prompt explicitly states
that allowed limitations can be compatible with satisfaction but inability to
verify cannot. The response validator enforces status consistency, not factual
truth. The saved criteria contain condition, evidence expectation and allowed
limitations, without distinct per-criterion pass/fail/undetermined boundary fields.
Such fields are not inherently necessary; clear conditions could express the same
boundaries. Here the business wording leaves too much ambiguity about provisional
scores and what a pending confirmation can excuse. This is a design weakness to
test, not an established sole cause: R45 against a stated50-74 band already violates
the existing scoring criterion, yet the actual grader accepted it. Clarifying
outcome boundaries should preserve the grader's choice of investigation strategy,
distinguish compliant disclosure of genuinely missing inputs from unsupported
definite scores, and not require rejecting every useful bounded draft. No runtime
prompt, standard, schema or saved trial result was changed for this clarification.
