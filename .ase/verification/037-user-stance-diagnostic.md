## Interpretation boundary and next useful investigation

This experiment addresses observable user-stance sensitivity under complete short
inline evidence. It does not identify a model's training objective, prove the absence
of sycophancy, or validate native autonomous retrieval and report acceptance. The
criteria make the numeric bands and unknown distinction very explicit; repeated
variants are paired controls, not independent task domains. Missing valid responses
remain missing, even when their readable labels appear reasonable.

The existing Task034 real-report false pass remains unresolved: relevant rain/rating
evidence reached that agent, yet it failed to establish and apply the correct numeric
constraint. Task036's post-hoc answer after explicit counterexamples is not autonomous
detection. The next useful investigation should return to that real failed evidence
and separate evidence association/context effects from the ability to make a negative
judgment, with a frozen counterpart and a control that must pass. Do not infer that
adding stronger negative language, forcing rejection, or another reviewer resolves
the trunk. A targeted change must later demonstrate real-report gain and no invented
defects/unknown coercion before claiming improvement. No such change or promotion is
part of Task037.
